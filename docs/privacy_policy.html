Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 15, 2026 • Effective Date: August 15, 2026

Welcome to LifeScore ("LifeScore", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing transparent information about our data practices in full compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and the Google Play Developer Policy.

Summary in Plain English: Your data belongs to you. We do not sell your personal information. We store your habit progress in secure cloud databases for multi-device sync, process video clips locally on your device, and allow you to export or permanently delete your account data at any time with one tap.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide and improve LifeScore:

  • Account Credentials: Name, email address, and unique User Identifier (UID) when you sign in via Google Authentication or Email.
  • Psychometric & Habit Data: 130-question assessment responses, assigned Hero Archetype, custom habit routines, streak records, coin ledger transactions, and 8-dimension scores (Fitness, Health, Career, Wealth, Learning, Mental Health, Relationships, Social Life).
  • Camera & Microphone (Micro-Vlogs): Video and audio recordings captured via CameraX are stored strictly on your device's private application storage for daily vlog stitching. No raw footage is transmitted to external servers without your explicit intent.
  • Device & Usage Diagnostics: Device model, OS version, and anonymous performance telemetry to detect application crashes and improve responsiveness.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Core Gamification Engine: Calculating your real-time 0–1000 LifeScore, managing streak shields, and evaluating league rankings.
  • Personalized AI Coaching (Google Gemini): Supplying anonymized dimension scores to generate daily executive briefs, habit suggestions, and weekly audits.
  • Cross-Device Backup: Syncing your offline SQLite database to encrypted Google Cloud Firestore collections.
  • In-App Purchases: Verifying Pro subscription status via Google Play Billing.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner only with industry-leading infrastructure providers under strict Data Processing Agreements:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics)
  • Google Generative AI (Gemini API) (AI Coaching Advice)
  • Google Play Services & Billing (Purchase Entitlements)

4. GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Compliance

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and California enjoy full statutory privacy rights:

  • Right of Access & Portability: Export a full machine-readable JSON copy of your personal data directly in the app.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to Be Forgotten): Permanently wipe your account and all Firestore documents at any time.
  • Right to Rectification: Edit or update any profile attributes within Settings.
  • Non-Discrimination: We do not degrade app performance or charge penalties if you exercise your privacy rights.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

LifeScore is designed for general audiences and is strictly intended for individuals aged 13 years and older (or 16 years in certain European jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

6. Security Measures

All data transmitted between LifeScore and our servers is protected using TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest on Google Cloud infrastructure.

7. Account & Data Deletion Step-by-Step

To permanently delete your data:

  1. Open the LifeScore app and go to Settings ⚙️.
  2. Scroll down to Data Management & GDPR.
  3. Tap "Delete My Account & Wipe Cloud Data" and confirm.

All associated Firestore records and local databases will be permanently destroyed within 24 hours.
